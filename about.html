The project

Video editing should be accessible.

ClipForge is a free, browser-based experiment in making short-video editing simpler, especially when automated highlight tools miss the moments that actually matter.

Why we built ClipForge

Long recordings contain a lot of footage that nobody wants to watch twice. But choosing a strong clip is not the same as choosing the loudest, most chaotic or most colorful part of a recording. A good edit also needs context: who made the play, what happened just before it, and whether the scene has a meaningful ending.

We tested automatic highlight extraction with game recordings and saw the limitations first-hand. A tool can identify a busy team fight while completely missing that the player whose footage it is has already been eliminated. Instead of pretending this problem is solved, our initial web release puts the decision in the editor's hands.

What the current version does

The free studio opens local video files, lets you select and combine multiple segments from one recording, reframes them for vertical or square social video, draws a text headline, and exports from the browser. It requires no registration. Export happens in real time and available file formats depend on your browser.

What comes next

Our longer-term goal is to make highlight suggestions more dependable, make multi-source workflows easier, and let creators export better shorts with fewer clicks. Those capabilities are research directions, not promised features of this release.

Who it is for

Gamers editing their own recordings, streamers cutting long sessions into shareable moments, solo creators who prefer simple tools, and anyone who wants to learn the difference between selecting a clip and telling a story.

For a problem report or suggestion, use the contact page. We especially welcome feedback about clipping decisions, export compatibility and mobile usability.
