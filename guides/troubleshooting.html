Why your video won’t export.

Solve common problems with browser-based video editing: unsupported formats, silent exports, oversized files and slow renders.

5 min reading time

Why browser video exports behave differently

Desktop video editors usually rely on native media libraries. This version of ClipForge uses standard browser video playback, a canvas and the browser's MediaRecorder. That makes editing possible without uploading files or paying for a server, but it also means supported codecs, performance and export formats vary by device and browser.

The file downloaded as WebM—not MP4

That usually means your browser supports WebM recording but not MP4 recording through MediaRecorder. ClipForge checks support instead of saving WebM bytes with a fake .mp4 extension. Use an editor or converter that supports WebM if your publishing workflow requires MP4. A future version may add true conversion, but this release doesn't promise it.

The video is very large or export is slow

A two-hour 4K recording is expensive to decode and render in any browser. Start by making a shorter segment, close unnecessary tabs, and try a desktop device. The beta is designed for short exports; even if it can open a long recording, it may not finish exporting if device memory is insufficient.

The export has no audio

Confirm "Include original audio" is enabled. Check whether the source really has an audio track and is playing with sound. Some browser/codec combinations may be unable to capture the track. If the file is silent, try another browser or export the video without sound and add audio in a different editor.

The preview is black or frozen

The source codec may not be playable by the browser, even when its filename ends in .mp4. Test the file in a normal video tab. If it does not play there, convert it to a broadly compatible format (for example H.264 video with AAC audio in MP4) using a desktop editor before importing.

The render stops when switching tabs

Browser rendering happens in real time, not as a background processing job. Background tabs may be throttled to save resources. Keep the tab visible, disable aggressive power-saving modes while exporting and avoid suspending your laptop.

How to report a reproducible bug

Include your operating system, browser version, source resolution and format, selected clip length, chosen layout, whether audio was enabled, and what occurred. Example: "Windows, Chrome, 1920 × 1080 MP4, 12-second 9:16 Blur + fit, no audio, export hangs at 70%." Do not send private recordings unless you choose to.
