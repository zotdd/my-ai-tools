How it works

From raw file to a finished short.

Everything happens locally in your browser. There is no video upload or paid API required for the basic editor.

STEP 01

Open your recording

Import your own video or try the included sample. Find key points by scrubbing and previewing the footage.

STEP 02

Cut, frame & title

Add the moments in the right order. Choose 9:16, 1:1, or 16:9 and adjust the title, text size, zoom, and framing.

STEP 03

Export & publish

Render the selected clips locally and download the video. Export runs in real time and depends on browser codec support.
