Site policy

Privacy Policy.

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Local video processing

The ClipForge Studio in this release reads video you choose directly in your browser using a local file reference. It renders the preview and export in your browser. Your original video file is not uploaded when you use the standard editor. If you explicitly opt in to the DeepSeek AI beta, ClipForge samples and compresses still frames from the time range you select. These frames and the instructions you enter are transmitted to our backend and then to DeepSeek's API for analysis. Images may contain faces, names, or other information visible in your video. Avoid using private or confidential footage with the AI feature. The AI access code is sent to our server to authorize a request, and is not intentionally saved in your browser.

Information we do not request

You do not need to create an account or provide a name, password, or payment information to use the Studio. This version does not provide social login, server-side video storage, or community accounts. Optional AI requests are handled by DeepSeek as a third-party processor; consult DeepSeek's privacy practices before opting in. We do not intend to store sampled frames in a database.

Optional DeepSeek scene suggestions

The beta sends a limited number of compressed JPEG still frames, their timestamps, and your scene instructions via the ClipForge server to DeepSeek. The model provides approximate suggestions, which may be inaccurate. The feature is restricted with an access code during testing. Only enable it if you have the right to share those frames with an AI provider. See DeepSeek for provider information.

Hosting and technical data

Like most hosted websites, the hosting provider may process IP addresses, request timestamps, and browser or device information to deliver pages, handle abuse and maintain site security. The precise retention and logging behavior depends on the hosting provider chosen for deployment.

Cookies, analytics and advertising

This release does not intentionally install advertising trackers or analytics cookies through its own code. We have not installed Google AdSense ad tags in this release. If advertising, analytics or third-party embeds are added later, this policy will need to be updated and any legally required consent or disclosures implemented before activation.

Links to other websites

Guides and support pages may contain links to external sites. Those websites operate under their own privacy policies.

Contact and corrections

If you contact the site operator by email, the message and contact information you provide will be used to respond to your inquiry. To request a correction or raise a privacy question, email support@ai-free-tools.com.
