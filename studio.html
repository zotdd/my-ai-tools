How is this free?

The editor processes your video inside your own browser instead of sending large recordings to a server. Export takes approximately the same time as the final video, and demanding recordings may need a powerful computer.

Why might I get a WebM instead of MP4?

Different browsers support different recording formats. The editor checks which formats your browser can create and chooses MP4 when possible. If not, it uses WebM and clearly labels the file. There is no fake MP4 conversion.

Does ClipForge automatically find highlights?

DeepSeek Vision beta can suggest rough scene ranges from still frames sampled in a manually chosen 90-second window. This is not continuous video understanding, and suggestions may be wrong. You can also select clips entirely manually without AI.
